Lead Dispatch • New Delhi / Kathmandu

PM Balendra Shah Concludes India State Visit with NPR 240B Package, 8 MoUs & Ayodhya–Janakpur Rail Launch

Prime Minister Balendra Shah and Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi adopted the "Viksit Bharat and Samunnat Nepal" Joint Vision at Hyderabad House today, announcing NPR 56 billion in reconstruction grants, NPR 184 billion in energy lines of credit, bidirectional B345 air-route talks, and access to Kandla and Paradip ports. Back in Kathmandu, Energy Minister Biraj Bhakta Shrestha served as Acting PM while the Special Court summoned 93 defendants in the NPR 734.9 million land-swap case and the Federation of Nepali Journalists demanded withdrawal of the Press Council Ordinance.
